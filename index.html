Ask for access

The members' reading room is private to Psanctuary's people. To read it:

  1. Write to the church at info@psanctuary.org or through the contact form, and say you would like to read the drafts. Include your GitHub username if you have one (a free account is enough).
  2. An officer says yes; the technical steward, Christopher W, sends a GitHub invitation to Psanctuary and its readers team.
  3. Accept the invitation (the email from GitHub, or the bell icon on github.com), turn on two-factor authentication, and open the reading room. You read it in your browser; nothing to install.

Bringing an AI helper is welcome and has its own short steps: how Psanctuary welcomes members and their helpers.
